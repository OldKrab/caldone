# A clearer look at your food photos

CalDone now sends photos with a detail setting that helped GPT-6 Sol recognize foods it previously confused in our tests.

- Better recognition of tested food and packaging photos, including photos reopened during a conversation.
- GPT-6 Sol and Luna appear in the model picker when available to your connected account.
- Your selected model, thinking level, and original photos stay unchanged.

## What changed

Codex photo requests now explicitly use high image detail instead of automatic detail. This setting is separate from the model's thinking level. The model catalog uses the verified Codex 0.156.1 compatibility version; available models still come from your account, not a fixed list.

In a controlled comparison of four food photos with three repetitions per setting, GPT-6 Sol identified the intended food in 12 of 12 high-detail responses versus 9 of 12 automatic-detail responses. Astra scored 12 of 12 with either setting. Luna scored 3 of 12 with either setting, so this change does not resolve its recognition problems. These small-sample results are not a guarantee for other photos or nutrition estimates.

The release also reconciles the saved workspace with the latest published code, retaining existing AI comments, background dish additions, and other shipped fixes. Repository guidance and the CI test command are aligned with that workspace.

## Updating

Install this update over your existing CalDone installation. No data migration or reconnection is required.
