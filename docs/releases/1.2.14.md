# Keep your words—and see the AI’s assumptions

See the assumptions behind a meal estimate in a separate AI comment, while your original note stays in your own words.

- Keep details such as an estimated amount of oil visible in the meal card, even before a calorie estimate is ready.
- Let the assistant update or remove its comment after you clarify a detail; inspect the change and undo it when needed.
- Carry AI comments with your meal history when exporting and importing a backup.

## Existing records and validation

Updating adds an empty optional comment field to existing records. It does not rewrite your notes, reconstruct previously overwritten text, or generate comments for old meals automatically.

Validation includes 236 automated tests, TypeScript checks, two real model turns that saved and revised a comment without changing the original note or nutrition, and Android component checks in Russian at 130% font scale. The Android captures use synthetic data in a compatible preview runtime; they are not a full end-to-end test of the release APK.
